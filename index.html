Where it comes from

Games run inside official embeds from CrazyGames, Scratch and publishers' own builds, credited to each creator. Where a publisher blocks embedding, the game links out to its official page instead — nothing is mirrored here.

Film and TV metadata comes from TMDB — “This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.” Trailers play from YouTube, and music plays through Spotify and SoundCloud official embeds.
