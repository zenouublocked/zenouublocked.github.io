4. Rights that cannot be excluded

Nothing in this page excludes, restricts or modifies any guarantee, condition, warranty, right or remedy you have under the Australian Consumer Law — including the consumer guarantees in sections 51 to 59 of the Competition and Consumer Act 2010 (Cth) — where those apply and cannot lawfully be excluded.

If a guarantee applies and the failure is not a major failure, the operator's liability is limited, at its option and to the extent the law allows, to resupplying the service or paying the cost of resupply. Because the service is free, that remedy will usually be the cost of resupply, which is nil.

Nothing here limits liability for fraud, for deliberately misleading conduct, for death or personal injury caused by negligence where such a limit is prohibited, or for anything else the law does not allow to be limited.
