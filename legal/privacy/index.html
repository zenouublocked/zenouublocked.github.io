Privacy Policy

ZenouMath has no accounts and no analytics. This page is the complete list of what is collected anyway, and by whom.

1. Who is responsible

The operator named in the contact page is responsible for the small amount of personal information described here. Whether the operator is an "APP entity" bound by the Australian Privacy Principles is a question of fact and law — see what needs legal review. This policy is written to the standard of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles, whether or not that Act formally applies.

2. What is not collected

This is the shortest list to trust, so it comes first:

  • No accounts. No name, email address, password or profile.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers. No Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no session recording, no fingerprinting script.
  • No first-party cookies. The site sets none of its own.
  • No server-side viewing history. What you watch, play or save is not recorded on the server.
  • No selling or renting of personal information. Ever, to anyone.

3. What is collected

Two things, both ordinary:

Server request logs

The web server that serves the pages records ordinary request metadata: the requested path, the time, the HTTP status, the user-agent string your browser sends, and the IP address the request came from. These logs exist to keep the site up and to detect abuse. They are retained for [RETENTION PERIOD] and then deleted or rotated.

API usage counters

The optional metadata API in front of TMDB keeps in-memory counters (requests served, cache hits and misses) and a keyed cache of upstream responses. These are operational statistics, not profiles: there is no per-visitor identifier attached to them and they are cleared when the process restarts.

4. What stays in your browser

These keys live in localStorage on your device. They never reach a server, and clearing site data deletes them.

KeyHolds
zenoumath.consentThe agreement version you accepted and the date you accepted it. Nothing else.
zenou_prefsAppearance: theme, glow, radius, density, motion, background, accent hue.
zenou_lib and relatedFavourites, playlists, ratings, notes, recently opened items, hidden games.
Doorway templatesProxy address templates you add yourself. They stay local because public proxy instances are short-lived.
__gsuite_theme_pref_v2A cached copy of the doorway menu, so a dead mirror does not leave a blank door.

Because none of it is transmitted, the operator cannot read it, export it or restore it for you. That cuts both ways: it is private, and it is not backed up.

6. Cookies

The site sets no first-party cookies. Third-party embeds on a page — YouTube, Spotify, game builds — may set their own as soon as they load. If you want to block them, block third-party cookies in your browser, or use a content blocker; the site continues to work without them, though embeds may not.

7. Why the little we hold is held

  • Server logs — to serve the site, diagnose faults and resist abuse (APP 3, directly related to operation).
  • Acceptance record — to keep the agreement and to avoid asking you again (stored on your device, not collected).
  • API counters — to keep upstream request volume inside TMDB's limits so the site stays available.

8. Disclosure and overseas storage

Personal information is not sold, rented or shared for marketing. It may be disclosed where the law compels it, or to a hosting provider strictly as part of serving the site. Logs may be held on infrastructure located outside Australia, most likely the United States or the European Union, depending on where the site is hosted. The countries involved are [HOSTING LOCATIONS] and should be confirmed against the host actually used.

9. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. There is no account database to breach because there are no accounts. That reduces risk; it does not eliminate it. No system is perfectly secure, and no guarantee of security is given.

10. Your rights and complaints

Under the Australian Privacy Principles you may ask what personal information is held about you, ask for it to be corrected, and ask for a correction to be associated with the record. In practice, almost everything is in your own browser — but the request can still be made.

Send a request to the privacy address on the contact page. We will respond within a reasonable period, ordinarily 30 days. If you are not satisfied with the response, you may complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

11. Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. It has no registration, so it could not collect a child's details even if it wanted to. Where the site is used by a student, local school policy on acceptable use is a matter between the student and the school.

12. Changes

This policy carries the site's agreement version, currently 2026-11-07. A material change bumps it and the first-visit gate asks again. The date at the foot of this page records the last edit.
