2. What to include

  • The exact URL on this site, not just the section name.
  • What you saw and what you expected instead.
  • For a copyright matter, the contents listed in the takedown process.
  • For a privacy matter, what information you want and how you want it delivered.

Do not send identity documents, passwords or anything else sensitive. None of it is needed to answer any of the matters above.

3. Response times

Corrections are ordinarily handled within a week. Copyright notices are handled on an interim basis within [N] business days. Privacy requests are answered within a reasonable period, ordinarily 30 days. There is no telephone support, no live chat and no ticket number.

4. What needs review

Four items in this legal centre genuinely require an informed decision before the documents can be relied on. They are listed in full at the top of the Legal centre:

  1. whether the operator is an APP entity under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth);
  2. whether any safe-harbour protection under section 115A of the Copyright Act 1968 (Cth) is available or claimed;
  3. how Australian authorisation law applies to the site's links, frames and embeds;
  4. whether the liability cap survives the unfair-contract and consumer-guarantee provisions.
These are questions for an Australian lawyer, not for a template. Nothing in this legal centre is a substitute for that advice, and nothing in it claims that the site is fully compliant with any regime.
