A door is any proxy that takes a target URL in its own address. Add yours with a template: {url} is the address, {enc} is its encoded form, {b64} is base64.

    Why the sites were failing

    StreamEx, Tubi, Plex, Roku, HydraHD and Movy all send X-Frame-Options or a CSP frame-ancestors list. That is enforced by the browser, so no page can override it. Separately, StreamEx's own player, player.zxcprime.xyz, no longer resolves in DNS, so playback there fails even in a normal tab.

    Why the players work

    The VidNest, VidFast, VidRock, VidUp and VidCore family, plus VA Player, VidSrc, VidLink, MultiEmbed and 2Embed, all serve a film from /movie/<id> and an episode from /tv/<id>/<season>/<episode>, and none of them send a framing header. They are the layer the catalogue sites sit on, so this page uses them directly.

    How the shelf is graded

    scripts/verify-movies.mjs follows every redirect and grades the final response, because a 200 on the way to a survey farm is not a working site. Sold domains, dead hosts and framing blocks are recorded, not hidden, and the check date rides on every record.

    About the doors

    A door is a proxy you trust, added here with a template, used only when you press it on a site that refuses framing. Public proxies die constantly, so the shelf never ships a hardcoded one. Nothing is mirrored or re-hosted here either way.
