How To Fish

How To Fish is a fishing game that combines traditional angling with direct harpoon fishing and open-water exploration. Players can catch different fish, travel by boat, meet quirky characters, and improve their fishing skills through hands-on gameplay.

REEL IN YOUR NEXT CATCH IN HOW TO FISH

Every fishing trip in How To Fish gives you a chance to practice your timing and control. Cast your line, wait for a bite, and hook the fish at the right moment before carefully reeling it in. You must manage the line tension throughout the process, as pulling too hard or allowing the line to become too loose can make landing a fish more difficult. If you want a different approach, switch to a harpoon and test your aiming skills. Beyond fishing, you can explore the shoreline and surrounding water, travel by boat, and encounter different fish and unusual characters along the way.

Master the Art of Rod Fishing

  • Cast your line: Send the bait into the water and wait for a fish to bite.
  • Time the hook: React at the appropriate moment to secure your catch.
  • Manage line tension: Maintain a balance between pulling and releasing to avoid making the reeling process more difficult.
  • Reel carefully: Bring the fish closer without losing control of the line.

Aim Carefully with Your Harpoon

  • Choose your target: Look toward the fish you want to catch.
  • Prepare your throw: Hold the action input to get ready to launch the harpoon.
  • Release at the right moment: Use accurate timing to improve your chances of hitting the target.
  • Switch fishing methods: Alternate between the rod and harpoon to enjoy different approaches to catching fish.

Explore Beyond Your Favorite Fishing Spot

  • Travel by boat: Move across the water and discover more places to explore.
  • Discover different fish: Encounter a variety of fish during your fishing trips.
  • Meet quirky characters: Enjoy unexpected encounters that add personality to the experience.
  • Improve your skills: Keep practicing both fishing methods as you explore the shore and surrounding waters.
