Chapters

How it was made

Real captures

Every screen in the trailer is a screenshot of the live site, taken at 1600 × 1000 from Chrome. Nothing is a mock-up.

Motion graphics

Every frame is a Three.js scene rendered headlessly in Chrome: real 3D cards, a perspective grid, camera moves and kinetic type, 2,160 frames at 1080p.

Sound

Neural narration over a score synthesised from scratch at 120 BPM, side-chain ducked under the voice, then cut to the same clock as the picture.

Rebuild it

The whole pipeline lives in trailer/. Run python build_trailer.py after capturing new shots.
