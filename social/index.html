Where it comes from

Every practice set runs inside this page, embedded from its publisher's own build: HoodaMath, CrazyGames, Scratch or the developer's own server. Nothing is re-hosted and credit stays with each creator.

Lecture pages show metadata from TMDB and frame other people's players. Audio plays through the Spotify and SoundCloud official embeds.
